## 1. What they are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser's local storage) are small files a site saves on your device to remember your preferences or to measure how it is used.

## 2. What we use

**Technical (necessary).** The site does not work properly without them. They do not need your consent.

| Name | What it is for | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `apo-tema` | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you delete it |
| `apo-cookies` | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you delete it |

**Analytics and marketing.** They are loaded only if you accept them.

| Name | Provider | What it is for | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_fbp`, `_fbc` | Meta (Meta Pixel) | Measure which ads bring visits and be able to show you APO ads later on | Up to 90 days |

We use no other analytics tools. Meta may process this data under its own policy.

## 3. How to change your choice

- The first time you visit you will see a notice with **Accept**, **Reject** and **Settings**.
- You can change it whenever you want from **Cookie preferences** in the footer.
- If you withdraw the marketing permission, we delete the Meta cookies and the page reloads without the Pixel.
- You can also block or delete cookies from your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari). Rejecting non-essential cookies does not affect the main operation of the site.
